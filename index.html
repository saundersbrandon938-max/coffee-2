For your call with Miss Heather

Lead with what she’s already built.

This is a warm, specific pitch—not a generic website sale. The goal of the call is to earn permission to show the concept.

“Hi Miss Heather, I’m Brandon, a local web designer. I came across The Brick and Mortar and noticed you’ve built an incredible 4.9-star reputation with 143 reviews, but people searching for you don’t have a simple website where they can see what makes the café special, find your location, and call you directly. I put together a homepage concept using your real pastry case so you can see what that could look like. Could I show it to you?”

Why this lands

  • It opens with her success, not a criticism.
  • It names the gap clearly: searchers need a direct path to the café.
  • It asks for a look, not a purchase.

What the concept proves

A mobile-first homepage can turn the café’s strong reviews and visual appeal into calls and visits. This concept gives the address and phone number immediate priority, uses a real photo from the café, and keeps changing menu details honest with a “call for today’s selection” prompt.
